Before you go

Book-ahead order

4+ weeks out

Ba Na Hills

  • Buy online rather than at the gate — online sellers discount against the walk-up price.
  • Adult tickets run about 950,000–1,000,000₫ for non-residents, covering the cable car, Golden Bridge, French Village and the themed areas.
  • New for 2026: a ticket stays valid for three consecutive days with unlimited cable car rides, and entry uses face scanning after the first pass.
2 weeks out

Hội An cooking class

  • All three classes are small and family-run, so they fill early. Message on WhatsApp or Zalo to hold a spot.
  • Flag dietary needs when you book — several hosts happily rework the menu with notice.
  • Most include hotel pickup within Hội An, not from Đà Nẵng. Confirm which.
1 week out

The Hội An ride home

  • This is the one leg that bites people. Grab is easy going down to Hội An and genuinely hard to get back late at night.
  • Pre-book a private car with a waiting driver for the day, or agree a return pickup time with whoever drove you down.
  • Book direct rather than through the hotel desk — hotel markups on this run are steep.
Same week

Splurge dinner

  • Gypsy Rooftop and The View both want a reservation, especially for a river- or city-facing table.
  • Mention if it's a special occasion — both have form for quietly decorating the table.
  • Mộc Quán and Thèm Hải Sản don't take bookings far out; just arrive before 18:00 or expect a wait.
On the ground

Practical notes

Getting around

Grab, and the yellow bus

  • Install Grab before you fly and add a payment method. Xanh SM (electric) is a fixed-fare alternative worth having too.
  • Short city hops run roughly 40,000–80,000₫. Anything across town rarely tops 120,000₫.
  • Yellow Bus #1 to Hội An costs about 30,000₫ a head and picks up on Trần Phú near Hàn Market — three minutes from your door. Slow (about 90 minutes) but absurdly cheap.
  • If you find a driver you like, swap numbers. Booking them direct for later runs is normal and usually cheaper.
Weather

Early October is the turn

  • Central Vietnam's wet season is starting, and early October sits inside typhoon season. Expect heat, humidity and the chance of heavy afternoon rain.
  • Build in the flexibility to swap Day 3 and Day 4 — Ba Na Hills is miserable in fog and rain, and Hội An handles a grey day far better.
  • The Cham Museum is the reliable indoor fallback, and it's a ten-minute walk from the hotel.
Money & connectivity

Cash, SIM, tipping

  • Grab a tourist eSIM or a Viettel/Mobifone SIM at the airport — you need data for Grab to be useful at all.
  • Carry small notes. Entrance gates at Marble Mountains and most street food stalls are cash-only.
  • Tipping isn't expected but is appreciated; spa staff in particular.
Pacing

What to actually cut

  • Six days is comfortable for this list only if you accept one slow day. Day 5 is that day — resist filling it.
  • Marble Mountains takes 2.5–3 hours done properly, not the 45 minutes people budget for.
  • If something has to go, it's the second river cruise or the night market — both are pleasant rather than essential.
